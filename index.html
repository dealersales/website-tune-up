06 — Straight answers

Kicking the tyres.

My site is on Click Dealer / Starkwood / Autoweb / Car Dealer 5. Can you still do it?
Usually yes, but it’s a different job and we’ll quote it as one. On a Carro or WordPress build we work where the site lives. On a proprietary platform — Starkwood’s AutoHub, Click Dealer, Autoweb, Car Dealer 5, Dealerweb, an Auto Trader website package or a franchise template — there’s no code to hand over and no design to edit, so the only way to rebuild is on our platform. That means every page, not two, and the domain and stock feed move with you. It’s quoted as a build rather than a tune-up — but it still comes back in five working days. If you’re inside a contract we build it and schedule the switch for when your notice ends. What we won’t do is quote you for two pages and tell you in week two that it’s actually a migration.
Do I lose my stock feed or start uploading cars twice?
No. Your feed keeps running — Carro, Auto Trader, or your own DMS — and we point the new site at it. If you currently type your stock in by hand we'll say so and quote a feed setup separately, but you won't be double-keying anything.
Will my Google rankings drop?
That's the right thing to worry about, and it's why the job includes redirects. We keep the URLs that are earning traffic wherever the platform allows it, and put 301s on everything else. Nobody can promise a rankings guarantee, and any agency that does is lying to you — what we can promise is that we don't throw away pages that are already being found.
Do I get to see it before it goes live?
Yes — you see it and you can say no. What we don't do is rounds of design ping-pong. We stick to your brand, your colours and your stock, so the argument about which shade of grey is somebody's taste, not our work. That's how a five-day job stays a five-day job.
What about the finance wording on my site?
Finance content is regulated. When we rebuild, we check what's on the page against the FCA register and flag anything naming the wrong company, a lapsed permission or a missing representative wording — it's one of the most common things we find on dealer sites. We don't write regulated copy or approve it for you; your compliance sign-off stands. We just won't silently rebuild a page that carries the wrong legal entity on it.
What if something breaks afterwards?
If a layout falls apart or something stops rendering in the first 30 days, we fix it free. We only ever touch the front end, so whatever's behind it — your stock system, your CRM, your finance calculator — is untouched.
Can I get my money back?
You don't pay to find out. Send the URL, we look at it, and if we don't think we can genuinely improve it we say no and there's nothing to refund because nothing was charged. Once work starts it's a fixed job at an agreed price, and the fee comes straight off anything bigger you do with us in the next six months.
Do you do the rest of the pages, the ads, the Google listing?
Yes, all of it — extra pages and the Google Business Profile tune-up are quoted with the job, and we run monthly websites, stock feeds, directory listings and paid search for dealers across the country. But the tune-up is deliberately small, so you can see how we work before you spend anything bigger.
